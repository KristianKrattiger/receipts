Receipts: what a vendor claims, what independent sources report, and which
claims nothing corroborates.

Every quote is verified to be an exact substring of a page we actually
fetched. An LLM proposes the pairings; a deterministic gate discards anything
it cannot find in the bytes.

**One row that no single page contains.** Tesla's Vehicle Safety Report says
FSD "improves U.S. road safety by over 80%." A Hacker News thread on the same
ledger says robotaxi crashes in Austin were "4x worse than humans." Both
halves are verbatim. Open the offsets in the report JSON and slice them out of
the fixture if you want to check.

**The finding the 10-K made visible.** The same Tesla run reads three of the
company's own documents. Split the rows by which one they cite:

- Tesla 10-K (FY2024): 8 rows, **all corroborated**
- Vehicle Safety Report: 5 divergent, plus unverified and corroborated

The SEC filing agrees with Tesla's critics while the marketing page
contradicts them. That split is not on any one page — it only appears when
both registers sit under one subject.

**Unverified is a finding.** 6 of 26 Tesla rows have no independent source
either way. Most tools silently drop those. Receipts names them.

**Browsers only.** The sources that matter — Reddit, G2, status histories,
regulator pages — refuse datacenter fetch. Stealth cloud browsers with proxy
egress are why two sides of the ledger exist at all. Solari also offers
sandboxes and desktops; this uses neither. Padding the stack to look more
complete would be decoration reviewers who build this infrastructure spot
immediately.
